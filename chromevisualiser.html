Chrome Audio Visualiser

 A Chrome extension that brings audio spectrum visualisation to your browser. Inspired by the desktop Audio Visualiser, this extension visualises audio directly in Chrome — no extra software needed. Features:
  • Live audio spectrum visualisation in the browser
  • Customisable colours, bar count, and style
  • Works with any tab playing audio in Chrome
